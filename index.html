Usage Guidelines

  1. Maintain clear space around the monogram.
  2. Use dark logo variations on light backgrounds.
  3. Use the reversed logo on dark backgrounds.
  4. Preserve the logo's original proportions.
  5. Use the 32 × 32 px favicon in browser tabs.
